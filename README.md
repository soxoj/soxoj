### Hi, I’m Soxoj 👋

**I build tools and methods for people who investigate on the Internet 🔎**

- Most people know me for [Maigret](https://github.com/soxoj/maigret) — an OSINT tool that turns a username into a dossier of accounts.

- For the past 10 years, I've been exploring and automating digital investigation methods: from classic cybersecurity to social media investigations ([list of my tools](https://github.com/stars/soxoj/lists/my-osint-tools)). ⚙️

- I founded the ["OSINT mindset" community](https://t.me/s/osint_mindset) and several OSINT projects, including [SOWEL](https://sowel.soxoj.com/about) — a knowledge base for social media intelligence based on weakness analysis.

- I'm always open to **collaborating with other developers**: whether you have an idea, want to build something together, or just need someone to review, support, or help spread the word about your project. 🤝

- Currently, I'm Chief Product Officer at [Social Links](https://sociallinks.io/), where I work on advanced investigation products and help turn OSINT expertise into software for real-world cases.
